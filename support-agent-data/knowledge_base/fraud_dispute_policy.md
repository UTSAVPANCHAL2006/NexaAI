# Fraud and Dispute Policy

## Unauthorized transactions
- Report unauthorized debits within 24 hours for maximum protection under bank policy.
- After report, block affected cards, UPI, and net banking credentials immediately.
- Bank may issue provisional credit within 10 working days for card fraud pending investigation.

## Dispute process
1. Customer raises dispute via app, phone, or branch with transaction reference.
2. Case ID assigned; acknowledgment SMS/email sent.
3. Investigation with NPCI / card network / beneficiary bank (7-45 days depending on type).
4. Final resolution: permanent credit, denial with reason, or partial settlement.

## Dispute timelines (indicative)
- UPI wrong debit / failed but debited: 7-15 working days.
- Card POS dispute: 30-45 days per network rules.
- NEFT wrong transfer: depends on beneficiary bank; 15-30 days typical.

## Chargeback (cards)
- Customer must dispute within 60 days of statement date.
- Evidence required: receipt, communication with merchant, police report for fraud above threshold.

## Phishing and social engineering
- Bank never asks for OTP, PIN, CVV, or full card number on call or chat.
- Report phishing links to fraud@bank and delete suspicious apps.
- Account may be temporarily restricted after fraud alert until customer verifies identity.

## Liability
- Customer negligence (sharing OTP): customer liability may apply.
- Reported lost card before unauthorized use: zero liability for subsequent fraud on card.
- SIM swap fraud: report to telecom and bank immediately; investigation case opened.

## Escalation
- Unresolved disputes beyond SLA can be escalated to banking ombudsman as per RBI scheme.
- Keep case ID and all correspondence for escalation.

## Law enforcement
- For large fraud, file FIR; bank cooperates with police on official request.
- Do not share investigation details with third parties.
