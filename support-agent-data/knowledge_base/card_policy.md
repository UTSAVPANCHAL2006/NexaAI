# Card Policy

## Debit and credit cards
- Debit cards are linked to savings or current accounts; credit cards have a separate credit limit and billing cycle.
- Card activation requires OTP verification on the registered mobile number.
- Contactless (tap) payments are enabled by default up to INR 5,000 per transaction; above that, PIN is required.

## Card status
- Active cards can be used for domestic and international transactions based on card settings.
- Blocked cards cannot be used for any transaction until unblocked or replaced.
- Expired cards must be replaced; transactions on expired cards are declined.

## Blocking and hotlisting
- Cards can be blocked instantly via mobile app, internet banking, or customer support using last 4 digits verification.
- Temporary block can be lifted within 24 hours; permanent block requires replacement card issuance.
- Lost or stolen cards must be reported immediately; liability for unauthorized use may depend on reporting time.

## Lost / stolen reporting
- Report lost or stolen cards immediately to prevent fraudulent usage.
- After report, the card is hotlisted and a replacement card is dispatched in 5-7 business days to the registered address.
- Emergency replacement at select branches may be available for premium customers (nominal fee may apply).

## Replacement
- Standard replacement delivery: 5-7 business days.
- Replacement fee: waived for first replacement per year on select card variants; otherwise INR 200-500 plus taxes.
- New card PIN is generated separately; activate via ATM or app.

## Limits
- Daily ATM withdrawal and POS limits are configurable within bank-defined maximums.
- International usage can be enabled or disabled per card from the app.
- Credit card cash advance limits are lower than purchase limits and attract higher interest.

## Charge disputes on cards
- Dispute incorrect card charges within 60 days of statement date.
- Provisional credit may be applied after preliminary review for unauthorized transactions.

## Customer responsibilities
- Never share CVV, OTP, or PIN.
- Enable transaction alerts for all card usage.
- Report suspicious SMS or calls asking for card details.
