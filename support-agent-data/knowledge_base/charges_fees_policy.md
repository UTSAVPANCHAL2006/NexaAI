# Charges and Fees Policy

## Account maintenance
- Regular savings accounts: zero balance maintenance charge on select salary and digital-only variants.
- Non-maintenance of average monthly balance (AMB) may attract fee of INR 150-600 plus GST depending on city tier and account type.
- Current accounts: AMB requirements higher; charges apply if AMB not met.

## Transaction charges
- UPI: free for customers on most retail accounts (merchant charges may apply to businesses).
- NEFT: free via mobile/internet banking; branch-assisted NEFT may incur INR 2-25 plus GST based on amount slab.
- IMPS: free up to INR 1 lakh per day on digital channels for most savings accounts.
- RTGS: INR 25-50 plus GST for branch; often free on digital channels.

## Card fees
- Annual fee: debit card free on basic variants; premium debit/credit cards INR 500-5000 per year.
- Card replacement: INR 200-500 plus GST (first replacement may be waived).
- International transaction markup: 3.5% on foreign currency transactions on debit/credit cards.
- ATM withdrawals: 5 free transactions per month at own-bank ATMs; beyond that INR 21 plus GST per transaction.

## Cheque and demand draft
- Cheque book: first 25 leaves free per year; additional leaves charged.
- Stop payment: INR 100-200 per cheque plus GST.
- DD issuance: INR 50-500 based on amount and channel.

## SMS and alerts
- SMS alert charges: INR 15-25 per quarter on some account variants; free on digital premium accounts.
- Email alerts: free.

## Loan-related charges
- Processing fee: 0.5%-2% of loan amount for personal loans.
- Prepayment / foreclosure: nil or 2-4% on select floating rate products as per sanction letter.
- Late payment penalty: as per loan agreement.

## GST
- All service charges attract applicable GST (currently 18% unless exempt).

## Fee revision
- Schedule of charges is published on the bank website; 30 days notice for material changes where required by regulation.

## Waiver requests
- Fee reversal for genuine service failures (failed ATM dispense, wrong debit) can be raised via case ID; decision within 7 working days.
