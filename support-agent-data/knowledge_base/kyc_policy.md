# KYC Policy

## Purpose
Know Your Customer (KYC) verification is mandatory under RBI guidelines for opening and operating bank accounts, wallets, and high-value services.

## Full KYC requirements
- Valid PAN (Permanent Account Number) is mandatory for all individual accounts.
- Aadhaar-based e-KYC or offline Aadhaar verification is required for full KYC tier.
- Address proof: utility bill, rent agreement, or government-issued document not older than 3 months.
- Recent photograph and signature specimen for branch-verified accounts.

## KYC tiers
- **Full KYC:** No restriction on balance (subject to product rules); all channels enabled.
- **Partial / minimum KYC:** Lower transaction limits; some channels restricted until upgrade.
- **Under review:** Documents submitted; account operational with reduced limits until verification completes.

## Verification timelines
- e-KYC via Aadhaar OTP: usually instant to 24 hours.
- Document upload via app: 2-3 business days.
- Branch submission: 3-5 business days.
- Re-KYC for dormant or high-risk profiles: annual or event-based.

## Missing documents
- Customers receive a checklist of pending items in the mobile app under Profile > KYC.
- Common missing items: Aadhaar XML, PAN image, address proof, signature.
- Accounts with pending KYC beyond 90 days may face debit freeze until completion.

## Updates and changes
- Name change requires marriage certificate / gazette notification and updated PAN.
- Address change requires new address proof; mobile number change requires branch visit or video KYC.
- NRIs require passport, visa, and overseas address proof.

## Video KYC
- Available for savings account opening for eligible customers during banking hours.
- Stable internet, original PAN and Aadhaar, and well-lit environment required.
- Agent will capture live photograph and verify documents on video call.

## Privacy
- KYC data is stored securely and used only for regulatory compliance and account servicing.
- Customers can request a copy of submitted KYC documents at the branch.
