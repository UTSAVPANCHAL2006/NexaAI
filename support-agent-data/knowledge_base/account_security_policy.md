# Account Security Policy

## Authentication
- Login requires customer ID / registered mobile and password or MPIN.
- Two-factor authentication (OTP on registered mobile) mandatory for high-risk actions: new payee, limit change, card unblock.
- Biometric login on mobile app is optional and device-specific.

## Password and PIN rules
- Net banking password: minimum 8 characters, mix of upper, lower, number, special character; change every 90 days recommended.
- UPI PIN and card PIN must not be shared; bank staff never ask for these.
- After 3 wrong PIN attempts, card may be blocked at ATM; reset via app or branch.

## Device binding
- UPI and mobile banking register trusted devices; new device requires OTP and sometimes cooling period.
- Deregister old devices when changing phones.

## Session security
- Auto logout after 5 minutes of inactivity on internet banking.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking without VPN.
- Install apps only from official app stores.

## Alerts and monitoring
- Enable SMS/email/push for all debits and credits.
- Review mini statement weekly for unknown transactions.
- Instant block options in app for cards and net banking.

## Account freeze
- Bank may freeze account for suspicious activity, court order, or incomplete KYC.
- Customer must visit branch or complete video KYC to remove freeze.

## Nominee and death claim
- Nominee registration recommended for all accounts.
- Death claim requires death certificate and claimant KYC at branch.

## Data privacy
- Bank does not sell customer data to marketers.
- Data shared with regulators and payment networks only as required by law.
- Customers can update marketing consent in profile settings.

## Reporting security incidents
- Call 24x7 fraud helpline immediately for unauthorized access.
- Change passwords after any suspected compromise.
- File police complaint for large unauthorized transfers.
