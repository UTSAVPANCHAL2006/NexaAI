# Refund and Reversal Policy

## UPI refunds
- Failed UPI with debit: automatic reversal within 24-48 hours; no separate refund request needed in most cases.
- Successful UPI to merchant: refund only through merchant; bank can assist with dispute if merchant refuses.
- Customer must provide UTR, date, amount, and merchant name for merchant refund disputes.

## Card refunds
- Merchant refunds credit to card within 5-10 business days depending on acquirer.
- If refund not received after 15 days, raise dispute with transaction and refund proof from merchant.

## NEFT / IMPS reversals
- Failed NEFT/IMPS: no debit or automatic credit within 1-3 business days.
- Wrong account credit: beneficiary bank recall process; not guaranteed; may take 15-30 days.

## Duplicate debits
- Verified duplicate debits refunded within 5-7 working days after internal verification.
- Both transaction references required.

## Loan and EMI refunds
- Excess EMI debit adjusted in next cycle or refunded to source account within 7 days.
- Foreclosure surplus credited within 3-5 business days after loan closure.

## Interest and fee refunds
- Service failure (failed ATM dispense, double charge): fee reversal within 7 working days on approval.
- Goodwill refunds at branch manager discretion for documented errors.

## Refund modes
- Refunds always to original payment source (same account or card) except where regulation requires otherwise.

## Non-refundable items
- Government taxes, stamp duty, and third-party convenience fees may not be refundable by bank.
- Merchant cancellation policies apply for marketplace purchases.

## Tracking refunds
- Refund status visible in app under transaction details once processing starts.
- Reference number provided for NEFT/IMPS credit refunds.
